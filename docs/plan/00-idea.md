# P0 One-Page Idea & Appetite (`00-idea.md`)

## One-Liner (P0.01)

For independent computational neuroscientists, formal-methods researchers, and biophysics learners who need nanoscale human synaptic bouton models that are numerically trustworthy instead of hand-waved HTML scripts, `human-synapse-bouton-lab` delivers a four-language (Lean 4.35.0, Rust, TypeScript, 50-digit Python), multi-kernel-verified interactive workbench and two reproducible AMS-LaTeX white papers that prove core biophysical invariants and openly test pre-registered stochastic hypotheses.
