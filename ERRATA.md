## E01 — Pre-Registered Hypothesis H3 (`40 Hz`) Refuted (`F09`, `C05`)
- **Date:** 2026-11-23 (`v1.0.0`)
- **Finding Class:** `error`
- **Affected Artifacts:** `PREREGISTRATION.md`, `CLAIMS.md` (`C05`), White Paper 1 §4
- **What Happened:** `PREREGISTRATION.md` (committed at `a1919f2` prior to execution) predicted that mitochondrial OxPhos (`950,000 ATP/s`) would maintain $>90\%$ bouton ATP over `60 s` at `40 Hz`. *[Corrected 2026-11-26: the registration does not state `950,000 ATP/s` or any other model constant; those constants were set in code after registration. See "2026-11-26 statement audit" below.]* Upon execution, resting V-ATPase $\text{H}^+$ leak (`4,000 ATP/s`) plus `40 Hz` firing cost (`40 * 32,000 = 1,280,000 ATP/s`) totaled `1,284,000 ATP/s`, exceeding mitochondrial supply (exact critical frequency $f_{\text{crit,mito}} = (950,000 - 4,000)/32,000 = 29.5625\,\text{Hz}$). Both treatment and control arms depleted to `0.0` (`p_Holm = 1.0000`, Cliff's $\delta = 0.00$).
- **Correction:** Preserved verbatim as **refuted / retracted** (`C05` in `CLAIMS.md` and White Paper 1). Logged a separately labeled post-hoc exploratory comparison `H3b` at `20 Hz` in `DEVIATIONS.md` (`1.00` vs. `0.00`, $p_{\text{Holm}} = 0.0005$).

## Withdrawal, Correction & Retraction Policy (Playbook Edition 1.3 §8 Item 39)

1. **Append-Only Transparency:** Confirmed errors, falsified pre-registered hypotheses (such as `E01`/`H3`), and scope corrections are NEVER silently deleted or force-pushed out of git history; every defect is recorded permanently in `ERRATA.md`, `CLAIMS.md` (with `retracted` status where applicable), and `CHANGELOG.md`.
2. **Severe-Error Retraction Trigger:** If an external checker, independent replicator, or mathematical review identifies an unrepairable flaw in any headline theorem, dataset, or white-paper claim:
   - Within 72 hours of verification, a `RETRACTION NOTICE` banner is added to the top of `README.md`, `ERRATA.md`, `CLAIMS.md`, and the affected manuscript PDF.
   - A new semantic version release is tagged so CERN Zenodo mints a superseding archival version while preserving the prior version DOI intact for historical provenance.
   - Downstream registrations (`0thernes-research-archive` and author ORCID Works metadata) are updated to reflect the correction or retraction status publicly.
3. **Reporting Channel:** File a GitHub issue on [`0thernes/human-synapse-bouton-lab`](https://github.com/0thernes/human-synapse-bouton-lab/issues) or email `0_0@0thernes.art` (ORCID `0009-0006-8168-9648`).
